# Ridely — Master Program Rules (DRAFT v0)

Last updated: 2026-11-13. Status: first draft from web research. **VERIFY** means sources do not agree, or we found no current source. Call the program and update the field, the date, and the source.

## 1. Crosstown (Princeton)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Operator | Center for Modern Aging Princeton (CMAP, was Princeton Senior Resource Center). Rides are given by GMTMA's RideProvide. Princeton pays part of the cost. |
| Who can use it | Princeton residents who register with CMAP. Age limit: **VERIFY** (no source gives one; RideProvide uses 65+ or visually impaired). |
| Service area | **VERIFY** — Princeton and nearby places (Princeton Medical Center in Plainsboro, Princeton Fitness & Wellness). |
| Cost | $3 each one-way ride, paid with a voucher (**VERIFY**, older source said $2). Free rides to Penn Medicine Princeton Medical Center and to Princeton Fitness & Wellness (medical rehab). |
| How to book | Register with CMAP first. Book a few days before the trip (**VERIFY** exact notice). |
| Phone | CMAP 609-751-9699 (**VERIFY** — one NJ 211 listing gives 609-924-7108 and calls 751-9699 a fax). |
| Notes | 594 Princeton residents were enrolled (June 2025). |

## 2. RideProvide (GMTMA)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Operator | Greater Mercer Transportation Management Association (GMTMA), 15 Roszel Rd, Ste 101, Princeton |
| Who can use it | Adults 65+, and visually impaired adults of any age, who live in the service area. |
| Service area | Mercer County, Plainsboro, south Montgomery (**VERIFY**). |
| Cost | Yearly membership $40 (single) / $60 (couple). Each ride costs more for more "zones" (**VERIFY** zone prices). |
| How to book | Become a member first. Then call to book (**VERIFY** notice). |
| Phone | 609-452-5144, Mon–Fri 8:30–4:30 (**VERIFY**; some sources give 609-452-5140). Website: rideprovide.org |

## 3. Mercer County TRADE

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Operator | Mercer County (TRADE Division, Human Services / ADRC) |
| Who can use it | Mercer County residents age 60+, or with a disability. |
| Service area | Mercer County (**VERIFY** out-of-county medical trips). |
| Cost | No fee. Donations accepted. |
| How to book | You can register by phone when you book the first ride. Book at least 1 week before (**VERIFY**: other sources say up to 14 days before). Have ready: date, time, destination address and phone, return time. |
| Phone | 609-530-1971. Calls often go to voicemail — leave a message. |

## 4. NJ Transit Access Link (ADA paratransit)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Operator | NJ Transit |
| Who can use it | People whose disability stops them from using NJ Transit local buses. A phone interview decides eligibility. Not based on age. |
| Service area | Near NJ Transit local bus routes (federal ADA rule: ~3/4 mile from a route, during bus hours). **VERIFY** for Princeton. |
| Cost | **VERIFY** (ADA limit: max 2× the bus fare). |
| How to book | Apply first (phone interview). Then book at least 1 day before (**VERIFY**: NJ Transit held a hearing on changing the booking window). App available. |
| Phone | Reservations 973-491-4224 (option 2). |

## 5. Medicaid rides (NJ FamilyCare NEMT, by ModivCare)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Operator | ModivCare, for NJ Medicaid / NJ FamilyCare |
| Who can use it | NJ Medicaid / NJ FamilyCare members, for medical visits covered by Medicaid. |
| Service area | New Jersey |
| Cost | Free |
| How to book | Call at least 2 business days before (**VERIFY**: some health plans say 3 days). Have ready: NJ FamilyCare ID, pickup address, provider name, address, phone, appointment date and time, special needs. Online: member.modivcare.com |
| Phone | 866-527-9933. Deaf / hard of hearing: 866-288-3133. "Where's my ride": 866-527-9934. |
| Notes | Not for emergencies — call 911. Ridely must NOT ask for the Medicaid ID. It tells the user to have it ready when they call. |

## 6. Princeton Muni Transit (free bus, replaced FreeB)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who can use it | Anyone. Free. |
| Schedule | 2021 data: weekdays, 30-minute loop, Mon/Tue/Thu 2–5 pm, Wed/Fri 4–7 pm. **VERIFY** — may have changed. |
| Notes | Fixed route. Stops at senior housing. Track with the TripShot app. Good for local errands, not for out-of-town doctors. |
